FiloSign – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02 October 2026

1.Introduction

FiloSign (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the FiloSign mobile application (the “App”), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. This policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

2.Information We Collect

  • Account data. When you create an account, we store the email address and the username you provide.
  • Authentication data. We keep the minimum credentials and tokens needed to sign you in on multiple devices and to send transactional emails such as password-reset links.
  • Anonymous usage events. We gather aggregated, non-identifying data — for example, which lessons you start or complete and how long you spend in practice sessions — to improve the App.
  • Camera frames (on-device only). The camera-based sign-detection feature accesses your device camera only while the practice screen is open. Frames are processed entirely on-device and are never written to disk, uploaded, or shared with any server.

3.How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: authenticate you, sync your progress across devices, and deliver lessons.
  • Safety & security: detect abuse, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Communicate: send transactional emails for things like password reset and account changes.
  • Improve the App: analyse anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to understand which features are working and where users struggle.

4.Camera Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • The App requests camera permission only when you access the practice screen and when taking a quiz.
  • No frames, images, or derived biometric data are stored, transmitted, or shared with any server. All sign detection occurs locally on your device.
  • You may revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings. Doing so will disable the sign-detection feature but will not affect other parts of the App.

5.Third-Party Services

We rely on a small set of trusted service providers. The App uses Supabase for authentication and account management. Supabase handles email/password sign-up, sign-in, one-time codes, and password-reset flows, receiving only your email, username, and authentication tokens. Supabase’s privacy policy applies to the authentication data it processes on our behalf. No personally identifiable information is shared with any other third party.

6.Data Retention & Deletion

Account data (email and username) is retained for the lifetime of your account and is permanently erased within 30 days after you request deletion via the in-app Settings → Delete Account option, or by contacting us (see #10).

7.Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your personal data.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

We process personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules and regulations.

8.Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. On-device model inference keeps camera data off the network entirely, and authentication traffic is encrypted in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. The revised version will be posted in the App with a new “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10.Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: FiloSign Privacy Team Email: privacy@filosign.app

End of Privacy Policy
